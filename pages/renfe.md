## 🎫 Exclusive 5% Discount on Renfe Tickets

CinC 2026 registered attendees can benefit from a **5% discount** on Renfe train tickets to Madrid for the conference. This discount applies to AVE (high-speed) and Long-Distance (Larga Distancia) services.
{: .text-justify}

---

## How to Claim Your Discount

To request your Renfe discount, please follow these steps:
{: .text-justify}

1. **Register for CinC 2026**: You must be a registered attendee of the conference. If you haven't registered yet, please do so through the [Registration page](../registration).
{: .text-justify}

2. **Fill out the Discount Request Form**: Complete the form below with your registration details. Once verified, you will receive a **discount code** via email that can be applied when purchasing your Renfe ticket.
{: .text-justify}
<p class="notice--info">
  <strong>Note:</strong> Please ensure that the name you provide matches the one used in your CinC 2026 registration. The discount code will be sent to the email address you provide below.
</p>

<p><font size="4"><center><a href= "https://forms.gle/detyeJu5yTkUkq3t5" target="_blank">Renfe Discount Request Form</a></center></font></p>
{: .text-justify}

*Please allow 3–5 business days for the organizers to process your request and send you the discount code.*



3. **Book your Renfe ticket**: See tutorial [www.renfe.com/viajeros/tarifas/eventos.html](https://www.renfe.com/viajeros/tarifas/eventos.html){: target="_blank"} or by presenting this printed document proviede to you by the conference at any Renfe sales point (stations with in-person sales and travel agencies).
{: .text-justify}

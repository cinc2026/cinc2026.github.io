## 🚕 Exclusive 10% Discount on Cabify Rides

CinC 2026 attendees can benefit from a **10% discount** on Cabify rides in Madrid, on up to **4 trips**, with a maximum discount of **€20 per trip**, valid across **all Cabify categories**.
{: .text-justify}

<p class="notice--info">
  <strong>Validity:</strong> Madrid, from <strong>September 20 to 24, 2026</strong>.
</p>

---

## How to Use Your Discount

1. **Open the Cabify app** (or download it from the [official Cabify website](https://cabify.com){: target="_blank"} if you don't have it yet).
{: .text-justify}

2. **Enter the promo code** `CINC2026` in the app's promo code section before requesting your ride.
{: .text-justify}

3. **Enjoy your ride** — the 10% discount (up to €20 per trip) is applied automatically, for up to 4 trips during the conference dates.
{: .text-justify}

<p class="notice--success">
  🎫 <strong>Promo code:</strong> <code>CINC2026</code> — the same code applies for every attendee, no individual request needed.
</p>

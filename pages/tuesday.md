Join us for the **CinC 2026 Welcome Reception** at the beautiful **Jardines de Cecilio Rodríguez**, one of the hidden gems of Retiro Park in Madrid. These exquisite Moorish-inspired gardens, tucked within the iconic Parque del Retiro — a UNESCO World Heritage Site — offer an enchanting setting to meet fellow attendees, unwind after the first day of the conference, and enjoy an evening of conversation in one of Madrid's most romantic outdoor spaces.

## 🎫 Exclusive Discount on Iryo Tickets for CinC 2026 Attendees

CinC 2026 registered attendees can benefit from a **€3 discount** on Iryo high-speed train tickets to Madrid for the conference.
{: .text-justify}

| Detail | |
|---|---|
| **Discount** | €3 off per ticket |
| **Valid categories** | All ticket categories |
| **Travel window** | 4 days before and 4 days after the conference (Sep 16–27, 2026) |
| **Availability** | Limited to 100 codes · one use per code |

---

## How to Claim Your Discount

To request your Iryo discount, please follow these steps:
{: .text-justify}

1. **Register for CinC 2026**: You must be a registered attendee of the conference. If you haven't registered yet, please do so through the [Registration page](../registration).
{: .text-justify}

2. **Fill out the Discount Request Form**: Complete the form below with your registration details. Once verified, you will receive a **discount code** via email that can be applied when purchasing your Iryo ticket.
{: .text-justify}

<p class="notice--info">
  <strong>Note:</strong> Please ensure that the name you provide matches the one used in your CinC 2026 registration. The discount code will be sent to the email address you provide below.
</p>

<p><font size="4"><center><a href= "https://forms.gle/Z6KcAZLMxskgGKhF6" target="_blank">Iryo Discount Request Form</a></center></font></p>
{: .text-justify}

*Please allow 3–5 business days for the organizers to process your request and send you the discount code.*

3. **Book your Iryo ticket**: Once you receive your discount code, visit [iryo.eu](https://iryo.eu){: target="_blank"} to book your ticket and apply the code at checkout.
{: .text-justify}

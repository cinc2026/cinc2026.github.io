## Become a Sponsor

We are pleased to invite you to support Computing in Cardiology 2026 (CinC 2026), which will take place from **September 20-23, 2026, in Madrid, Spain**. This annual conference is a premier scientific event that brings together researchers, engineers, and clinicians to discuss cutting-edge advancements in cardiac signal analysis, artificial intelligence in cardiology, and innovative digital health technologies.

Your company, known for its commitment to innovation and research in healthcare, would gain exceptional visibility as a sponsor of CinC 2026. The conference attracts world-renowned experts and promising young talents, providing a unique opportunity to showcase your brand and engage with key leaders in the field.
{: .text-justify}

We offer several sponsorship levels designed to align with your marketing and visibility goals, including opportunities to promote your company during scientific sessions, in official conference materials, and in the exhibition space.

**For detailed sponsorship information and packages, please download our sponsorship document:**
[**📋 Sponsorship Opportunities (PDF)**]({{'/assets/ppt/sponsors.pdf' | relative_url}}){: target="_blank" .btn}

### CinC 2026 - 53rd annual edition of CinC - in brief:
- **The leading conference** in technology applied to cardiology
- **Expected 400+ on-site** attendees from all over the world
- **Visitors represent** biomedical researchers, engineers, clinicians, and policymakers from across the industry and academia
- **Sessions and poster exhibition** provide an outlook into the emerging R&D
- **State of the art scientific programme** and great events for personal interactions

## Sponsorship Levels

We offer several sponsorship levels designed to align with your marketing and visibility goals.

### Bronze Partners
- Logos appearing on the conference website, social media profiles, and conference booklet
- **Fee:** 1,000€

### Silver Partners
- All benefits of the Bronze partnership
- Full participation in the scientific programme for one person
- Printed materials and small items (provided by the partner) included in the conference bags
- Placing company roll-up (provided by the partner) at the event venue
- **Fee:** 2,000€

### Gold Partners
- All benefits of the Silver partnership
- One table, two chairs, electricity, Wi-Fi in the conference foyer
- Placing company roll-up (provided by the partner) on the presenter's stages
- Invitation to the Gala dinner for one person
- **Fee:** 3,500€

### Platinum Partners
- All benefits of the Gold partnership
- Full participation in the scientific programme for two additional persons
- 15-minute presentation during lunch break to present your products and services (if interested)
- **Fee:** 6,000€
